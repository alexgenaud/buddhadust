Aṅguttara-Nikāya
II. Duka Nipāta
X. Bāla Vagga

Sutta 99

Bāla Suttaṁ

Fools

Translated from the Pali by Thanissaro Bhikkhu.

Sourced from dhammatalks.org
For free distribution only.

 

 

[99.1][pts][olds] "Monks, these two are fools.

Which two?

The one who takes up a burden that hasn't fallen to him, and
the one who doesn't take up a burden that has.

These two are fools."
