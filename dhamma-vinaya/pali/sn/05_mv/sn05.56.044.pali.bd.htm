Saṁyutta Nikāya
5. Mahā-Vagga
56. Sacca Saṁyutta
5. Papāta Vagga

Sutta 44

Kūṭāgāra Suttaṁ

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

 

[452]

[1][pts][bodh][than] Evam me sutaṁ:|| ||

Ekaṁ samayam Bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:|| ||

"Bhikkhavo" ti.|| ||

"Bhadante" ti te bhikkhū Bhagavato paccassosuṁ.|| ||

Bhagavā etad avoca:|| ||

"Yo hi bhikkhave, evaṁ vadeyya:|| ||

'Ahaṁ dukkhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
dukkha-samudayaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodha-gāminī paṭipadaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
sammā dukkhassantaṁ karissāmī' ti||
n'etaṁ ṭhānaṁ vijjati.|| ||

Seyyathā pi bhikkhave, yo evaṁ vadeyya:|| ||

'Ahaṁ kūṭā-gārassa heṭṭhimaṁ gharaṁ akaritvā uparimaṁ gharaṁ āropessāmī' ti||
ti n'etaṁ ṭhānaṁ vijjati.|| ||

Evam eva kho bhikkhave, yo evaṁ vadeyya:|| ||

'Ahaṁ dukkhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
dukkha-samudayaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodha-gāminī paṭipadaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ anabhisamecca||
sammā dukkhassantaṁ karissāmī' ti||
n'etaṁ ṭhānaṁ vijjati.|| ||

 

§

 

Yo ca kho bhikkhave, evaṁ vadeyya:|| ||

'Ahaṁ dukkhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
dukkha-samudayaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodha-gāminī-paṭipadaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
sammā dukkhassantaṁ karissāmī' ti||
ṭhāname taṁ vijjati.|| ||

Seyyathā pi bhikkhave, yo evaṁ vadeyya|| ||

'Ahaṁ kūṭā-gārassa heṭṭhimaṁ gharaṁ,||
karitvā uparimaṁ gharaṁ āropessāmī' ti||
ṭhāname taṁ vijjati.|| ||

Evam eva kho bhikkhave, yo evaṁ vadeyya:|| ||

'Ahaṁ dukkhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
dukkha-samudayaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodhaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
dukkha-nirodha-gāminī-paṭipadaṁ ariya-saccaṁ||
yathā-bhūtaṁ abhisamecca||
sammā dukkhassantaṁ karissāmī' ti||
ṭhāname taṁ vijjati.|| ||

[453] Tasmātiha bhikkhave,||
'idaṁ dukkhan' ti yogo karaṇīyo||
'ayaṁ dukkha-samudayo' ti yogo karaṇīyo||
'ayaṁ dukkha-nirodho' ti yogo karaṇīyo||
'ayaṁ dukkha-nirodha-gāminī paṭipadā' ti yogo karaṇīyo" ti.|| ||
