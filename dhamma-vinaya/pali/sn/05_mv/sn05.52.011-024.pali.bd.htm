Saṁyutta Nikāya
5. Mahā-Vagga
52. Anuruddha Saṁyutta
2. Sahassa Vagga

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

Sutta 11

Sahassa Suttaṁ

[11.1][pts] Evam me sutaṁ:|| ||

Ekaṁ samayaṁ āyasmā Anuruddho Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati Jetavane Anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.|| ||

2. Atha kho sambahulā bhikkhū yen'āyasmā Anuruddho ten'upasaṅkamiṁsu.|| ||

Upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā anuruddhena saddhiṁ sammodiṁsu sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sārāṇīyaṁ vīti-sāretvā eka-m-antaṁ nisīdiṁsu.|| ||

Eka-m-antaṁ nisinnā kho te bhikkhū āyasmantaṁ Anuruddhaṁ etad avocuṁ:|| ||

3. "Katamesaṇ āyasmā Anuruddho dhammānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulī-katattā mahābhiññataṁ patto" ti?|| ||

4. "Catunnaṁ khv'āhaṁ āvuso, sati-paṭṭhānānaṁ||
bhāvitattā||
bahulī-katattā||
mahābhiññataṁ patto.|| ||

Katamesaṇ catunnaṁ?|| ||

Idh'āhaṁ āvuso kāye kāy'ānupassī viharāmi,||
ātāpī sampajāno satimā||
vineyya loke abhijjhā-domanassaṁ.|| ||

Vedanāsu vedan'ānupassī viharāmi||
ātāpī sampajāno satimā||
vineyya loke abhijjhā-domanassaṁ.|| ||

Citte citt'ānupassī viharāmi||
ātāpī sampajāno satimā||
vineyya loke abhijjhā-domanassaṁ.|| ||

Dhammesu Dhamm'ānupassī viharāmi||
ātāpī sampajāno satimā||
vineyya loke abhijjhā-domanassaṁ.|| ||

Imesaṁ khvāham āvuso catunnaṁ sati-paṭṭhānānam||
bhāvitattā||
bahulī-katattā||
mahābhiññataṁ patto.|| ||

5. Imesañ ca panāhaṁ āvuso,||
catunnaṁ sati-paṭṭhānānaṁ||
bhāvitattā||
bahulī-katattā||
kappa-sahassaṇ anussarāmī" ti.|| ||

bahudhā pi hutvā eko homi,||
āvībhāvaṁ tiro-bhāvaṁ tiro-kuḍḍaṁ tiro-pākāraṁ tiro-pabbataṁ asajja-māno gacchāmi seyyathā pi ākāse.|| ||

Paṭhaviyā pi ummujjani-mujjaṁ karomi seyyathā pi udake.|| ||

Udake pi abhijjamāno gacchāmi seyyathā pi paṭhaviyaṁ.|| ||

Ākāse pi pallaṅkena kamāmi seyyathā pi pakkhīsakuṇo.|| ||

Ime pi candima-suriye evam mahiddhike evam mah-ā-nubhāve pāṇinā parāmasāmi parimajjāmi.|| ||

Yāva Brahma-lokā pi kāyena vasaṇ vattemī" ti.|| ||

vimuttaṁ vā cittaṁ 'vimuttaṁ cittan' ti pajānāmī" ti.|| ||
