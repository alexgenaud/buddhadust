Saṁyutta Nikāya
5. Mahā-Vagga
56. Sacca Saṁyutta
2. Dhamma-Cakka-Pavattana Vagga

Sutta 17

Avijjā Suttaṁ

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

 

[429]

[1][pts][bodh] Evam me sutaṁ:|| ||

"Atha kho aññataro bhikkhu yena Bhagavā ten'upasaṅkami.|| ||

Upasaṅkamitvā Bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā eka-m-antaṁ nisīdi.|| ||

Eka-m-antaṁ nisinno kho so bhikkhu Bhagavantaṁ etad avoca:|| ||

"'Avijjā avijjā' ti bhante, vuccati.|| ||

Katamā nu kho bhante, avijjā?|| ||

Kittā vatā ca avijjā-gato hotī" ti?|| ||

"Yaṁ kho bhikkhu, dukkhe aññāṇaṁ,||
dukkha-samudaye aññāṇaṁ,||
dukkha-nirodhe aññāṇaṁ,||
dukkha-nirodha-gāminiyā paṭipadāya aññāṇaṁ||
ayaṁ vuccati bhikkhu, avijjā,||
ettā vatā ca avijjā-gato hoti.|| ||

Tasmātiha bhikkhave,||
'idaṁ dukkhan' ti yogo karaṇīyo,||
'ayaṁ dukkha-samudayo' ti yogo karaṇīyo,||
'ayaṁ dukkha-nirodho' ti yogo karaṇīyo,||
'ayaṁ dukkha-nirodha-gāmin paṭipadā' ti yogo karaṇīyo" ti.|| ||
