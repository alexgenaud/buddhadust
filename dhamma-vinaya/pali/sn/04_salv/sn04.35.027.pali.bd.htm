Saṁyutta Nikāya
4. Saḷāyatana Vagga
35. Saḷāyatana Saṁyutta
§ I: Mūla-Paññāsa
3. Sabba Vagga

Sutta 27

Dutiya Aparijāna Suttaṁ

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

 

[18]

[1][pts][bodh] Evaṁ me sutaṁ:|| ||

Ekaṁ samayaṁ Bhagavā Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati Jetavane Anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.|| ||

2. Tatra kho Bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi|| ||

"Bhikkhavo" ti.|| ||

"Bhadante" ti||
te bhikkhū Bhagavato paccassosuṁ.|| ||

3. Bhagavā etad avoca:|| ||

Sabbañ ca kho bhikkhave abhijānaṁ,||
parijānaṁ,||
virājayaṁ,||
pajahaṁ,||
bhabbo dukkha-k-khayāya.|| ||

Kiñ ca bhikkhave sabbaṁ abhijānaṁ,||
parijānaṁ,||
virājayaṁ,||
pajahaṁ,||
bhabbo dukkha-k-khayāya?|| ||

 

§

 

Cakkhuṁ bhikkhave abhijānaṁ,||
parijānaṁ,||
virājayaṁ,||
pajahaṁ,||
bhabbo dukkha-k-khayāya,||
rūpe abhijānaṁ,||
parijānaṁ,||
virājayaṁ,||
pajahaṁ,||
bhabbo dukkha-k-khayāya,||
cakkhu-viññāṇaṁ abhijānaṁ,||
parijānaṁ,||
virājayaṁ,||
pajahaṁ,||
bhabbo dukkha-k-khayāya,||
cakkhu-samphassaṁ abhijānaṁ,||
parijānaṁ,||
virājayaṁ,||
pajahaṁ,||
bhabbo dukkha-k-khayāya.|| ||

Yam pidaṁ cakkhu-samphassa-paccayā uppajjati vedayitaṁ||
sukhaṁ vā||
dukkhaṁ vā||
adukkha-m-asukhaṁ vā tam pi abhijānaṁ,||
parijānaṁ,||
virājayaṁ,||
pajahaṁ,||
