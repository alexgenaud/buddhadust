Saṁyutta Nikāya
4. Saḷāyatana Vagga
36. Vedanā Saṁyutta
1. Sagāthā Vagga

Sutta 2

Sukhaya Suttaṁ

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

 

[204]

[1][pts][nypo][bodh] Evaṁ me sutaṁ:|| ||

Ekaṁ samayaṁ Bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:|| ||

"Bhikkhavo" ti.|| ||

"Bhadante" ti||
te bhikkhū Bhagavato paccassosuṁ.|| ||

3. Bhagavā etad avoca:|| ||

"Tisso imā bhikkhave vedanā.|| ||

Katamā tisso?|| ||

Sukhā vedanā||
dukkhā vedanā||
adukkha-m-asukhā vedanā.|| ||

Imā kho bhikkhave tisso vedanā ti.|| ||

 

[205] "Sukhaṁ vā yadi vā dukkhaṁ||
adukkha-m-asukhaṁ saha||
Ajjhattañ ca bahiddhā ca||
Yaṁ kiñci atthi veditaṁ|| ||

Etaṁ dukkhan ti ñatvāna||
mosadhammaṁ palokitaṁ.||
Phussa phussa vayampassaṁ.||
Evaṁ tattha virajjatī" ti.|| ||
