Saṁyutta Nikāya
4. Saḷāyatana Vagga
36. Vedanā Saṁyutta
1. Sagāthā Vagga

Sutta 5

Daṭṭhabba Suttaṁ

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

 

[207]

[1][pts][nypo][bodh] Evaṁ me sutaṁ:|| ||

Ekaṁ samayaṁ Bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:|| ||

"Bhikkhavo" ti.|| ||

"Bhadante" ti||
te bhikkhū Bhagavato paccassosuṁ.|| ||

3. Bhagavā etad avoca:|| ||

"Tisso imā bhikkhave vedanā.|| ||

Katamā tisso?|| ||

Sukhā vedanā||
dukkhā vedanā||
adukkha-m-asukhā vedanā.|| ||

Sukhā bhikkhave vedanā dukkhato daṭṭhabbā,||
dukkhā vedanā sallato daṭṭhabbā,||
adukkha-m-asukhā vedanā aniccato daṭṭhabbā.|| ||

Yato kho bhikkhave bhikkhuno sukhā vedanā dukkhato diṭṭhā hoti,||
dukkhā vedanā sallato diṭṭhā hoti,||
adukkha-m-asukhā vedanā aniccato diṭṭhā hoti,||
ayaṁ vuccati bhikkhave bhikkhu sammaddaso acchecchi taṇhaṁ,||
vāvattayī saṁyojanaṁ,||
sammāmān-ā-bhisamayā antam akāsi dukkhassā" ti.|| ||

 

"Yo sukhaṁ dukkhato adda||
dukkham addakkhi sallato||
Adukkha-m-asukhaṁ santaṁ||
addakkhī naṁ aniccato.|| ||

Sa ve sammaddaso bhikkhu||
parijānāti vedanā||
So vedanā pariññāya||
diṭṭha-dhamme anāsavo||
Kāyassa bhedā dhammaṭṭho||
saṅkhaṁ nopeti vedagu" ti.|| ||
