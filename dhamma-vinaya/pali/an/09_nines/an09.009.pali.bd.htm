Aṅguttara Nikāya
IX. Navaka Nipāta
I. Sambodha Vagga

Sutta 9

Puggala Suttaṁ

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

 

[372]

[1][pts][upal][olds] Evaṁ me sutaṁ:|| ||

Sāvatthi nidānaṁ.|| ||

"Nava ime bhikkhave, puggalā santo saṁvijj'amānā lokasmiṁ.|| ||

Katame nava?|| ||

Arahā,||
arahattāya paṭipanno,||
Anāgāmi,||
Anāgāmi-phalaṁ sacchi-kiriyāya paṭipanno,||
Sakad-āgāmi,||
Sakad-āgāmi-phala sacchi-kiriyāya paṭipanno,||
Sotāpanno,||
sot'āpatti-phala sacchi-kiriyāya paṭipanno,||
puthujjano.|| ||

Ime kho bhikkhave, navapuggalā santo saṁvijj'amānā lokasmin" ti.|| ||
