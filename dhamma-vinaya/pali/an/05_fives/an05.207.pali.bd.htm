Aṅguttara Nikāya
Pañcaka Nipāta
XXI. Kimbila-Vagga

Sutta 207

Yāgu Suttaṁ

Adapted from the 1995 edition of the digital version of the Sri Lanka Buddha Jayanti Tripitaka Series.

 

[250]

[1][pts] Evaṁ me sutaṁ:|| ||

Ekaṁ samayaṁ Bhagavā Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati.|| ||

Tatra kho Bhagavā bhikkhu āmantesi:|| ||

"Bhikkhavo" ti.|| ||

"Bhadante" ti te bhikkhū Bhagavato paccassosuṁ.|| ||

Pañc'ime bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā yāguyā.|| ||

Katame pañca?|| ||

Khudaṁ paṭihanti,||
pipāsaṁ paṭivineti,||
vātaṁ anulometi,||
vatthiṁ sodheti,||
āmāvasesaṁ pāceti.|| ||

Ime kho bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā yābhuyāti.|| ||
