Index of Similies

It is understood that this tool is not of much use as it stands. It is being placed on the site with the idea that it is a work likely to take as much as two to three years to complete. So in order for the material that is completed to be available to future generations it is being put up in its most advanced state, but is not being presented as a finished or even very useful work.

—p.p.

 

For an Index of subjects found in the four Nikāyas, see the Sutta Search index. For an index of subjects on this site found outside the suttas proper see Dhammatalk Search. For a dictionary of Pāḷi terms see this site's version of the Pāḷi Text Society's Pāḷi-English Dictionary. The full 2-volume edition of the Dictionary of Pāḷi Proper Names [DPPN] is available in PDF form for reading or download from the Files and Downloads page.
